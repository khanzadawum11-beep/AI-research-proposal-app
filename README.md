# AI-research-proposal-app
academic research assistant specializing in scientific research proposal development. Generate original, well-structured, academically appropriate research titles, objectives, problem statements, hypotheses, research questions, methodologies, and expected outcomes based on the user's topic. 
Problem Statement:Many students and early-career researchers struggle to prepare research proposals because they lack experience in academic writing and proposal structure. Writing research titles, objectives, problem statements, and methodologies often requires significant guidance and time.Research Proposal Helper AI solves this problem by providing an intelligent academic assistant that helps users generate professional proposal content quickly and efficiently.
Target Users:Undergraduate Students,Master's Students,PhD Scholars,Academic Researchers,University Faculty
Features:User Authentication,User Registration,Secure Login,Logout,Password Reset
Dashboard:Welcome Dashboard,Proposal Statistics,Recent Proposal History,Quick Navigation
AI Proposal Generator:Generate,Research Title,Background,Problem Statement,Research Objectives,Research Questions,Hypothesis,Suggested Methodology,Expected Outcomes,Keywords
AI Academic Assistant:The chatbot can:Improve research titles,Rewrite objectives,Improve academic writing,Suggest methodologies,Explain research gaps,Answer proposal-writing questions
Responsive Design:Works on,Mobile,Tablet,Desktop
AI Feature:Academic Proposal Generator
The application integrates Google Gemini AI to generate academically structured proposal content based on the user's research topic
